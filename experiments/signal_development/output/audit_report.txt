═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  BUG 1 (CRITICAL): Similar-month return lookup uses CONCURRENT not FORWARD returns
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Location: regime/run_kritzman_backtest.py line 114-116 (and run_roth7_backtest.py)

Code before fix:
  sim_er[a] = mean(asset_ret.loc[d, a] for d in sim.similar_dates)

This uses the return OF the similar month — what happened DURING that month.
But the intent is: "conditions look like month d, what happens NEXT?"
The correct lookup is: asset_ret.shift(-1).loc[d, a] — the return of the
month AFTER the similar month.

Impact: The signal was momentum-like (go long what did well in similar conditions)
rather than regime-predictive (go long what did well AFTER similar conditions).
This inflated results because momentum naturally has positive returns.

Fix applied: asset_ret_fwd = asset_ret.shift(-1); use asset_ret_fwd for lookups.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  BUG 2 (CRITICAL): Z-score at month T used for month T allocation
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Location: regime/run_kritzman_backtest.py line 109-110

The z-score at month T includes end-of-month T data (12-month change uses
val_T, rolling std includes val_T). At the START of month T, end-of-month
values are not yet known. Using z_data[T] for month T allocation is a
1-month look-ahead on the conditioning information.

Impact: The similarity assessment sees the current month's outcome before
allocating, giving it information about whether the month was good or bad.
This further inflated the Sharpe ratio.

Fix applied: z_data_lagged = z_data.shift(1); use z_data_lagged for similarity.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  BUG 3 (CRITICAL): Turbulence/AR monthly resample not shifted
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Location: regime/run_kritzman_backtest.py lines 82-85

turb_monthly = turb_pctl.resample("MS").last() — takes end-of-month value
and applies it to that same month's allocation. Same look-ahead as Bug 2.

Impact: Turbulence scaling used end-of-month stress levels to adjust that
month's allocation. During crisis months, the scaling correctly identified
stress (because it saw the stress happening), inflating protection metrics.

Fix applied: Added .shift(1) to all monthly resamples of daily indicators.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  BUG 4 (MODERATE): Absorption ratio degenerate with 5 assets / 1 component
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Location: regime/kritzman.py compute_absorption_ratio()

With 5 assets and n_components=1, the first eigenvalue captures 99.1%-99.9%
of total variance at ALL times. The AR z-score measures tiny fluctuations
around 0.997, producing inverted signals (AR was LOWER during GFC because
assets were all declining uniformly, reducing the first PC's relative share
vs the now-elevated second PC).

Impact: The AR signal was inverted during crises — it showed z=-2.61 during
peak GFC instead of positive. This made Config 3 accidentally protective
(it RELEASED constraints during crisis instead of tightening them), but for
the wrong reasons.

Fix applied: Changed n_components from 1 to 2. With 2 components for 5 assets,
the top 2 PCs explain a more meaningful fraction and the ratio is less degenerate.
