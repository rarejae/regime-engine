**Data:** same yfinance cache as SFE equal

## Hypothesis

Equal-weight SFE’s 1/3 gold sleeve is defensively heavy (mean gold exposure ~18%). If the main CAGR gap vs V19d is **equity weight when on**, not signal complexity, then capping gold at 10% (45/45/10) with identical binary Faber rules should raise return and terminal wealth — at the cost of Sharpe and MaxDD.

Weight choice is a priori (matches V19d’s gold sleeve size as an investor preference), not a search.

## Design

Identical to [[2026-07-22_sfe_simple_faber_equal]] except sleeve targets:

| Sleeve | Equal SFE | This test |
|--------|----------:|----------:|
| QLD (gate QQQ) | 1/3 | **45%** |
| SSO (gate SPY) | 1/3 | **45%** |
| GLD | 1/3 | **10%** |
| Off → cash | yes | yes |

Unchanged: classic 10-month Faber, binary on/off, monthly only, no CB / guards / score tiers.

## Key Diagnostics

1. **Classic leverage tradeoff, not free lunch.** +1.05pp CAGR and +$5.9 terminal vs equal SFE, but Sharpe −0.076 and MaxDD −11.6pp worse.
2. **COVID gets worse, not better.** −35.1% vs equal −28.7% and ≈QQQ −28.6%. More equity when the monthly lag fails amplifies the unprotected first crash month. Still no CB.
3. **Gold was buying Sharpe.** Cutting gold 33%→10% raises mean effective equity from 98%→132% — almost exactly V19d’s mean eff equity (~131%). That explains most of the return lift vs equal SFE.
4. **Still below locked V19d** on the 2002 window (15.4% / 0.72 / −35% vs 17.3% / 0.87 / −25%). Remaining gap is CB + score/guard machinery + path differences — not just gold weight.
5. **DCA now beats QQQ** ($3.30M vs $2.82M) — equal SFE lost that race; 45/45/10 wins it by putting more capital in levered equity when on.

## Interpretation

Gold weight is a **risk dial**, not a signal question. Equal 1/3 is the risk-adjusted principle floor. 45/45/10 is the wealth-seeking cousin of the same Faber engine — closer to V19d’s economic exposure, still missing V19d’s COVID/CB edge.

Does **not** falsify SFE. Confirms the earlier read: a large share of V19d’s CAGR vs equal SFE was “more equity when on,” not multi-SMA voting.

## Decision

Keep **equal SFE as the simplicity / Sharpe floor**. Treat **45/45/10 as the a priori wealth-tilted principle variant** (investor preference on gold, not an optimized parameter). Neither replaces V19d for production while CB remains untested on this skeleton.
