## Hypothesis

Adding V19d’s daily circuit breaker (close below all of SMA-126/200/252 → cash next session; monthly re-entry only) on top of a priori SFE 45/45/10 should improve COVID / fast-crash drawdowns without needing V19d’s score tiers or IVV guard. Expected cost: whipsaw drag in choppy markets.

## Design

| Layer | Rule |
|-------|------|
| Sleeves | 45% QLD / 45% SSO / 10% GLD |
| Monthly | Classic Faber 10-mo SMA on QQQ / SPY / GLD → ON/OFF |
| Off | Sleeve weight stays cash |
| **CB (new)** | Daily: signal asset close < all of 126/200/252 → that sleeve to cash **next session** |
| Re-entry | Next monthly Faber rebalance only |

Single new mechanism vs [[2026-07-22_sfe_45_45_10_gold_cap]]. CB definition matches V19d; exit timing is next session (aligned with live “next open,” slightly stricter than some same-day research sims).

## Key Diagnostics

1. **COVID hypothesis confirmed.** CB cuts COVID DD by **8.1pp** vs no-CB 45/45/10 (−27.0% vs −35.1%) and beats equal SFE / QQQ on that window.
2. **Full-sample MaxDD gets worse** because of **dot-com** (−53.5%): early warmup + aggressive CB exits into a multi-year decline/chop regime. The 2000 start remains hostile.
3. **Post-2002, CB is not free but is coherent:** ≈flat Sharpe, −1pp CAGR, better MaxDD. Matches the V19 lesson that CB→cash helps crash windows and costs whipsaws.
4. **Does not recover V19d.** 2002 window SFEv3: 14.4% / 0.72 / −33% vs V19d ~17.3% / 0.87 / −25%. CB alone on the SFE skeleton is insufficient; V19d’s remaining edge is still multi-factor (signal stack, path, guards).
5. **Live whipsaw is real.** 2026 YTD CB costs ~4pp vs no-CB — same class of pain as V19d’s Mar–Apr 2026 cycle.

## Interpretation

On the a priori 45/45/10 Faber engine, the daily CB is a **crisis insurance policy with a premium**:

- Pays in COVID (and modestly in 2022)
- Premium shows up as lower full-sample CAGR, worse 2000-start DD, and worse 2026 YTD

Equal-weight SFE without CB still wins Sharpe/MaxDD on the full sample. SFEv3 is the right test of “add only CB,” and the answer is: **useful for fast crashes, not a dominant upgrade to the principle floor.**

## Decision

- Keep **SFE 1/3** as Sharpe / simplicity floor  
- Keep **45/45/10** as wealth tilt without CB  
- Treat **SFEv3** as the documented CB overlay result — adopt for production only if prioritizing COVID-style crash cuts over whipsaw tax  
- Do **not** claim SFEv3 closes the gap to V19d
